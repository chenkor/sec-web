Desktop repo

What Android includes

Local vault

Your identity lives on the phone, locked with a password and optional biometrics. Nothing unlocks itself. No cloud backup.

Private DMs

One-to-one chats with end-to-end encryption that hides more than just the message text.

Invite channels

Private rooms you join by invite only. Create, invite, accept, or refuse.

Built-in Tor

Tor ships inside the app. Flip tor-only when you want every hop through the network.

Bluetooth mesh

When IP is unavailable, peers can still talk over Bluetooth.

Contacts

Add people with a SEC QR or a public key. Use disposable identities when a circle shouldn't share the same key.
