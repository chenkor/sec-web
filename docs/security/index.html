Security

Keep the channel yours.

SEC is for private messaging when you don't want a platform account in the middle. Local keys, optional Tor, Bluetooth when the network is gone.

No central account

There is no SEC login server. Your identity is a keypair on your device. If you lose the vault, you lose access. That's intentional.

Sealed at rest

Data stays encrypted until you unlock. After a while idle, the app locks itself again.

Hardened egress

Cleartext HTTP is blocked. You can force Tor. Relay certificates can be pinned with TOFU.

Device hygiene

Where the OS allows it, SEC stays out of recents. Vault material is not backed up to Android cloud.

SEC won't make you invisible to a global adversary. What it does give you is encrypted messaging with keys you hold, Tor if you want it, and a Bluetooth fallback when IP is dead.
